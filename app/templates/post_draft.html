How the value is calculated

Value per season = positional value × snap share × performance.

  • Snap share is the main driver: a team that plays a player trusts him. Games missed to injury are excused; benchings count.
  • Performance is a single-season grade (0–100, percentile among that season's regulars at the position) that scales value from 0.2× to 1.8×.
  • Positional value is what the NFL pays the position (median of the top 10 veteran contracts, as a share of the cap). It's the same for everyone at a position, so it only matters in the All Positions view.
  • Recent seasons count most. Quarterbacks: this season 5×, last season 2×, two seasons ago 1×. Other positions, whose play depends more on circumstances: 3×, 2×, 1×. The current season counts in full while it's in progress.

Each season cell shows snap share and that season's grade{% if value.seasons %}; {{ value.seasons[-1] }} is in progress{% endif %}. A small +N marks games missed to injury. Offensive linemen aren't graded yet.
