How the value is calculated

Score: 70 = playing like a full-time, market-rate starter at his position. Higher means he's outplaying that rate, and it's proportional: 105 is 1.5× the market rate, 140 is twice it, 35 is half. Because it's measured against each position's own market, a 70 QB and a 70 tight end are equally good relative to what their positions are worth.

Value per season = positional value × snap share × performance.

  • Snap share is the main driver: a team that plays a player trusts him. It's measured against a typical starter at his position, so a running back on 62% of snaps is as full-time as a quarterback on 94%. Benchings count against him.
  • Injuries are excused up to 25% of his team's games across his career (one bad injury year is forgiven). A game he left hurt (under half his usual snaps, then out the next week) is excused the same way. Beyond that, missed games count as games he didn't play.
  • Performance is a single-season grade (0–100, percentile among that season's regulars at the position) mixing efficiency with production per game, and it scales value from 0.2× to 1.8×.
  • Positional value is what the NFL pays the position (median of the top 10 veteran contracts, as a share of the cap). The score is measured against it; the All Positions view ranks by raw draft value (% of the cap), where it does matter, so quarterbacks lead.
  • Recent seasons count most. Quarterbacks: this season 5×, last season 2×, two seasons ago 1×. Other positions, whose play depends more on circumstances: 3×, 2×, 1×. The current season counts in full while it's in progress.

Each season cell shows snap share and that season's grade{% if value.seasons %}; {{ value.seasons[-1] }} is in progress{% endif %}. A small +N marks games missed or left injured. An asterisk on a grade means the season is in progress and, for non-quarterbacks, it's blended with his track record in proportion to games played (hover to see).

Offensive linemen aren't graded: free data has no individual blocking stats (pressures or sacks allowed), so they're listed without a value score.
